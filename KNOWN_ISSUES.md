# Known issues and submission boundaries

1. **Organiser pack unavailable.** Only generated synthetic data and illustrative policies were used. Official schema compatibility, planted-violation recall/precision and exact judge-output compatibility are unverified.
2. **Bounded policy interpretation.** Supported English patterns become reviewable drafts. Ambiguous prose, working-day calendars and unsupported requirements need clarification/configuration. Approval limits and routing are examples until policy owners validate them.
3. **Workflow roles are simulated.** Named synthetic staff and read-only audit context demonstrate the process; they are not enterprise identity, role authorisation or proof of separate authenticated reviewers.
4. **Device-local private records.** Imported datasets persist on the same device and support backup/restore, but do not synchronise across devices. Browser storage can be edited or cleared; it is not immutable enterprise audit storage.
5. **Replay is a demonstration.** Successive synthetic ingestion runs while the browser is open. There is no unattended enterprise scheduler or live Emirates integration.
6. **Metadata and date semantics.** Document checks inspect attached names, not authenticity/signatures. Timing uses calendar days; historical employee snapshots and date/time interpretations require validation.
7. **Anomalies are leads only.** Peer-relative outliers require enough comparable records and are not proof of a breach or misconduct. They are excluded from policy-violation exports.
8. **No automatic corrections or external routing.** Owner assignment and decisions are recorded in the app. There are no email notifications, employee messaging, or changes to HR systems.
9. **Hosted release is older.** Review the local repository. Enterprise deployment needs internal SSO/RBAC, scheduled ingestion, approved storage, monitored backup and central append-only audit retention.
10. **Demo recording.** The supplied demo is demo-video/demo.mp4. Runtime behaviour and the tests remain the source of truth for any capability not shown in the recording.
